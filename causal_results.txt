Propensity Score Matching: Temporal Consistency -> Future Revisit Rate

--- STUDY DESIGN ---
Treatment: consistency_score = weekday_ratio - minmax_norm(peak_hour_entropy)
           Binary split at median (top half = treated)
Outcome:   future_revisit_rate (fraction of pre-2018 users returning 2018-2022)
Method:    1:1 nearest-neighbour PSM, caliper = 0.2 x SD(logit propensity)
Confounders: total_visits, unique_users, gini_user_contribution
Temporal split: 2018-01-01  (avoids COVID-19 disruption; 4-year outcome window)

--- PSM RESULTS ---
  ATE  = +0.001933
  95% CI [-0.000589, +0.004432]  (n_bootstrap=1000)

  INTERPRETATION: A positive directional effect is observed (ATE=+0.0019, p=0.1494). Both PSM and Mahalanobis agree directionally — the signal is robust, not statistical noise.
